Common Questions

What practice owners ask before booking.

What kinds of practices do you work with?

Private-pay women’s wellness practices. Functional and integrative medicine, hormone and menopause care, holistic gynecology, concierge and direct primary care, cash-pay psychiatry and mental health, fertility and preconception care, pelvic floor physical therapy, lactation and postnatal care, and adjacent specialties. If your patients pay out of pocket because the work is worth it, we’re likely a fit.

Can you write copy that actually sounds like my practice?

Yes. Whether I’m writing it for you or guiding you through it, every page is written for the actual patient you’re trying to attract. The tone, the language, and the angle are all tailored to who your patients are and how they make decisions.

Will my site show up on Google for the services I offer?

That’s the point. Every service page is written and structured to rank for what your patients are actually searching. SEO is baked in from day one, not bolted on later.

Do you handle compliance, privacy, or industry-specific requirements?

Marketing websites generally don’t store sensitive patient data, so most compliance concerns (including HIPAA) live with your booking system, patient portal, or internal tools. If your practice has specific regulatory needs, we’ll talk through them and make sure the site supports them.

What if I already have a website or I’m launching something new?

Both work. I rebuild existing sites that aren’t pulling their weight, and I launch new practices from scratch. Book a Clarity Call and we’ll figure out which path makes sense for where you are.

How do I know which package is right for me?

That’s what the clarity call is for. We’ll talk through what you already have in place, what’s missing, and which tier makes sense for where you are right now. If you start with Seed and decide later you want me to build it, you can move up.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

You can, but most of my clients don’t want to. That’s what ongoing care is for. I handle the updates, the new service pages, the email tweaks, the seasonal changes. You stay focused on your patients.
