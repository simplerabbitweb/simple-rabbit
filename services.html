Common Questions

What practice owners ask before booking.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Service-based businesses whose clients pay out of pocket for high-quality work. Specialty practices, private-pay services, studios, and small businesses where the work speaks for itself. If your client base expects more, we’re probably a fit.

Can you write copy that actually sounds like my business?

Yes. I write every page before I design it, and I write to the actual client you’re trying to attract. The tone, the language, and the angle are all tailored to who your clients are and how they make decisions.

Will my site show up on Google for the services I offer?

That’s the point. Every service page I build is written and structured to rank for what your clients are actually searching. SEO is baked in from day one, not bolted on later.

Do you handle compliance, privacy, or industry-specific requirements?

Marketing websites generally don’t store sensitive client data, so most compliance concerns live with your booking system, client portal, or internal tools. If your business has specific regulatory needs, we’ll talk through them and make sure the site supports them.

What if I already have a website or I’m launching something new?

Both work. I rebuild existing sites that aren’t pulling their weight, and I launch new businesses from scratch. Fill out an inquiry and we’ll figure out which path makes sense for where you are.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

You can, but most of my clients don’t want to. That’s what ongoing care is for. I handle the updates, the new service pages, the seasonal changes. You stay focused on your clients.
