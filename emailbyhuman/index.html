How I help

I build your email system once, then help you keep it warm.

Here's the idea. When someone joins your list, downloads your freebie or fills out your contact form, the right emails go out to them automatically, written in your voice and pointed toward a reply or a booked call. You don't have to remember to follow up, because the follow-up is already written and waiting.

I write every email myself, after listening to how you actually talk about your work. Then I set it all up in your email platform, test it, and hand it over with a short video so you know what's running and where.

If you'd like, I can also stick around and write your newsletters each month, so your list keeps hearing from you long after the setup is done.

How it works

From first call to live emails

  1. Book a free call

    We talk for 20 minutes about your business, who you help, and where email fits. If it's not a good match, I'll tell you.

  2. A voice interview

    I ask you questions and you just talk. This is how I learn the way you explain things, so the emails end up sounding like you.

  3. I write and build

    I write your emails and set everything up in your platform, including the signup form, template and automations.

  4. You approve

    You read every email before it goes anywhere. Ask for changes during your revision round and I'll make them.

  5. It goes live

    I switch it on, test it, and send you the handoff video. From then on, new subscribers get your emails automatically.

Questions

Things people usually ask

Which email platforms do you work with?

Most of the popular ones for small businesses, including Flodesk, Mailchimp and Kit. If you already use something else, ask me on the call. If you don't have a platform yet, I'll help you pick one that fits.

Do I get to approve everything?

Yes. Nothing goes out until you've read it and said it's good to go.

Is this AI-written?

No. I write every email myself, based on our voice interview and how you naturally talk about your work.

What's not included?

A few things sit outside the setup and get quoted separately: creating the lead magnet itself (the freebie people sign up for), e-commerce or shopping cart automations, and moving your list from one platform to another. If you need any of those, I'll give you a price before we start.
