Leann Frank

Patient growth strategist for women's health practices. I help private-pay practitioners build consistent new patient flow without turning into marketers.

Leann Frank runs Simple Rabbit, a patient growth consultancy for women's health practices in Bergen County, New Jersey. She built the ACRR System, a four-stage framework covering Attract, Convert, Retain, and Refer, to give private-pay practitioners a clear, repeatable way to grow their practice without becoming marketers. She talks about patient acquisition, what actually moves the needle in private-pay healthcare marketing, and how to build something of your own from one skill and a laptop.

Topics I cover

How to turn one skill into a business

What it actually takes to go from knowing how to do one thing to getting paid for it. Where the first clients come from, how to price your time, and the real difference between a hobby and a business.

Getting found online without becoming a marketer

Most people who run a business never wanted to do marketing and were never taught how. This one covers the handful of things that actually move the needle: showing up in search, saying the right thing on your website, and getting the right people to call.

Attract, Convert, Retain, Refer: a simple way to grow

A clear way to look at where your business gets people, where it loses them, and what to fix first. Built for owners who want a plan they can follow, not a marketing degree.

Using AI tools to enhance your marketing efforts

AI is not going to replace your marketing strategy, but it will change how you execute it. This topic covers how to use AI tools practically and without the overwhelm: writing better content faster, showing up in AI-powered search results, and knowing which tools are worth your time and which are not.
