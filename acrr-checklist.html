The Real Problem

Most private-pay practices run on referrals and hope.

That works for a while. But referrals plateau. Slow months get harder to explain. You are not sure which part of your marketing is working, or if any of it is.

The ACRR framework breaks patient growth into four stages. Most practices have gaps in at least two. The practices that grow consistently, month after month, have all four covered.

The checklist tells you where you stand.

This might sound familiar

  • You have patients, but no reliable way to predict next month
  • Your website is live but you are not sure it is actually doing anything
  • Most new patients still come from word of mouth
  • You have put time into marketing but you are not sure what is working
  • Something is leaking patients. You just do not know where

What the Checklist Covers

Patient growth breaks down in four predictable places.

01

Attract

Are the right patients finding you?

Your website, your Google Business Profile, and your local search presence all work together to put your practice in front of patients who can pay what you charge. Most practices are invisible in at least one place. A few are invisible everywhere. The Attract section shows you exactly which.

02

Convert

Is your website getting patients to book?

Traffic is not enough. The Convert section scores your website's copy, social proof, booking experience, and ability to pre-sell your credibility before anyone picks up the phone. This is where most practices lose the patients they worked hard to attract.

03

Retain

Are you keeping the patients you have?

Retention gaps are the quietest and most expensive. Patients do not always tell you why they stopped coming back. The Retain section surfaces the places your practice experience might be losing patients before they ever get the chance to refer someone new.

04

Refer

Is your practice building its own referral pipeline?

The best source of new patients you have is the patients who already trust you. Most practices leave this entirely to chance. A few specific, repeatable actions change the whole picture. The Refer section shows you what you are missing and what to do first.
