How It Works

Three steps from first call to launched system.

01

Discovery and strategy

I start with a real conversation about your practice, your patients, and the system you actually need. From there, I map out the strategy: how patients will find you, what your website has to do, and how email keeps them engaged. You get a clear plan before any build work begins.

02

Build and consult

Depending on your package, I build the pieces that need building, the website, the email workflow, the conversion paths, and consult on the rest. You see the work as it comes together, with check-ins along the way.

03

Launch and care

I launch when the site is ready, not when a deadline says so. The day it goes live, your care plan starts. Hosting, security, updates, and content changes are handled from there.
