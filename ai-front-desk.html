Every missed call is a patient who booked somewhere else.

A private-pay practice runs on responsiveness. But the calls come in while you’re with a patient, and voicemail is where inquiries go to die. The website form gets answered hours later, after she’s already booked with someone faster. And the interest that comes in after hours or on a weekend waits until Monday, if it waits at all.

You never hear about the ones you miss. They just don’t call back. Hiring more front-desk help is expensive and hard to find. An AI front desk answers instantly, every time, day or night. That is the whole idea.
