How to Grant Admin Access to Your Accounts

Step-by-step instructions for adding Simple Rabbit as an admin on the most common domain registrar and email marketing platforms.

Before you begin: Have your designer's email address ready. If you're not sure which email to use, ask before you start. You should never need to share your password to grant access. All of the platforms below have a built-in invite or delegate system.

In this guide
  1. Domain registrars
    • GoDaddy
    • Namecheap
    • Google Domains (now Squarespace)
    • Network Solutions
    • Cloudflare
    • SiteGround
  2. Email marketing platforms
    • Flodesk
    • Mailchimp
    • Constant Contact
    • Kit (ConvertKit)
    • ActiveCampaign
  3. My platform isn't listed

Domain registrars

Domain access lets your designer update DNS records, connect your domain to hosting, set up email authentication, and make other technical changes without needing you to log in every time. Find your registrar below.

  1. Log in at godaddy.com
  2. Click your name or avatar in the top right corner
  3. Select Account Settings
  4. In the left sidebar, click Delegate Access
  5. Click Invite to Access
  6. Enter your designer's email address
  7. Under access level, select Manage (this gives full admin access)
  8. Click Invite

Your designer will receive an email and accept through their own GoDaddy account. You each retain your own login, and you can revoke access at any time from the same Delegate Access page.

  1. Log in at namecheap.com
  2. Click your username in the top right corner
  3. Select Profile
  4. Go to the Account tab and look for Sharing & Transfer or Account Access
  5. Enter your designer's email and grant full access

Note: Namecheap's interface updates fairly often. If these steps look different, search "shared access" in their Help Center or go to your Domain List, click Manage next to your domain, and look for sharing or access options within the domain settings.

Google sold Google Domains to Squarespace in 2023. If you originally purchased your domain through Google Domains, your account was automatically migrated to Squarespace. You now manage it at domains.squarespace.com.

  1. Log in at domains.squarespace.com (use your Google account if that's how you originally signed in)
  2. Click the domain you want to share
  3. Go to Settings or look for Permissions & Ownership
  4. Click Invite Contributor or Share Domain
  5. Enter your designer's email and set the role to Admin or Manager
  6. Send the invitation

If you purchased your domain directly through Squarespace (not Google Domains), the steps are the same but you start at account.squarespace.com instead.

  1. Log in at networksolutions.com
  2. Go to My Account or Account Manager
  3. Look for Account Security or Account Access
  4. Select the option to add an authorized contact or secondary user
  5. Enter your designer's email and assign full admin permissions
  6. Save your changes

Network Solutions also uses a Customer Service PIN for phone-based support. Once access is set up, share that PIN with your designer separately so they can get help from Network Solutions support if needed.

  1. Log in at dash.cloudflare.com
  2. From the account home, look in the left sidebar for Manage Account
  3. Click Members
  4. Click Invite Member
  5. Enter your designer's email address
  6. Select the Administrator role
  7. Click Continue and confirm the invite

Your designer will receive an email. They can accept with an existing Cloudflare account or create a free one. You can remove or change their access at any time from the Members page.

SiteGround is a web host that also sells and manages domains. If your domain lives inside your SiteGround account (rather than a separate registrar), use these steps.

  1. Log in at my.siteground.com
  2. Click your name or avatar in the top right corner
  3. Go to User Management
  4. Select the Collaborators tab
  5. Click Invite Collaborator
  6. Enter your designer's email address
  7. Set the appropriate access level and send the invite

SiteGround's collaborator system lets you control which sites and tools your designer can access, so you don't have to give full account access if you'd prefer not to.

Email marketing platforms

Email platform access lets your designer set up templates, build welcome sequences and automations, and manage your list structure. Find your platform below.

  1. Log in at flodesk.com
  2. Click your account name or avatar (usually in the bottom left corner)
  3. Go to Account Settings
  4. Look for a Team or Members section
  5. Click Invite Member or Add Team Member
  6. Enter your designer's email address
  7. Set the role to Admin
  8. Send the invite

Note: Flodesk's team features may depend on your plan. If you don't see a Team option under Account Settings, reach out to Flodesk support to confirm whether your plan includes it.

  1. Log in at login.mailchimp.com
  2. Click your profile icon or account name (bottom left or upper right depending on your layout)
  3. Go to Account & Billing or Account Settings
  4. Select Users
  5. Click Invite a User
  6. Enter your designer's email address
  7. Choose the Manager role for full working access
  8. Click Send Invite

Your designer will receive an email invitation. They can accept it even without an existing Mailchimp account.

  1. Log in at login.constantcontact.com
  2. Click your name or profile icon in the upper right corner
  3. Select My Account
  4. Go to User Management or Manage Users
  5. Click Add User
  6. Enter your designer's email address
  7. Set the role to Account Manager for full access
  8. Send the invitation

ConvertKit rebranded to Kit in 2024. If you use ConvertKit, your account is now at app.kit.com.

  1. Log in at app.kit.com
  2. Click your avatar or account name (usually in the lower left)
  3. Go to Settings
  4. Select Team or Team Members
  5. Click Invite Team Member
  6. Enter your designer's email address
  7. Set the permission level to Admin
  8. Send the invite
  1. Log in to your ActiveCampaign account
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon or your profile name)
  3. Select Users & Groups
  4. Click Add User
  5. Enter your designer's email address
  6. Set the role to Administrator
  7. Click Add User to send the invitation

My platform isn’t listed

Most platforms handle team access the same way. Look for any of these:

  • Settings, Account Settings, or Profile
  • Team, Members, Users, or Collaborators
  • An Invite or Add User button

Once you find it, enter your designer's email and give Admin or Manager level access, then send the invite. If you get stuck, take a screenshot of where you are and send it over. We'll figure it out together.
