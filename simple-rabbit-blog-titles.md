Titles are grouped by the kind of post they'd anchor, so you can pick based on what fits your editorial calendar (or what you can write fastest).

## A Few Notes

The strongest titles for SEO are the ones that target a specific buyer pain (7, 8, 13, 16) and the ones that hit clear search intent (1, 3, 5). If you're picking 5 to start, I'd grab one from each category above so you build topical breadth instead of writing six versions of the same post.

Most of these can be backed by content you already have in your head from client work. The ones that lean on Smooth Laser Center (titles 6, 7, 9, 11, 16, 25, 28, 29) are the easiest first drafts because the case study is already written.

A couple of these double as lead magnets if you want to gate them: title 19 ("So You Left Insurance") and title 20 ("First 90 Days Online") would both work as a free PDF in exchange for an email, which feeds The Hutch newsletter.
