UNSTUCK: Thought Process for Long Form Topic & Content

CHOOSING YOUR LONG FORM TOPIC: 

When choosing your topic or theme for your long-form content, start by asking yourself these questions to help find clarity and step out of overthinking.
What’s top of mind for my audience right now? What are they most excited about?
What challenges are they facing as they go about their day, today?
What do they believe to be true about their goal? What do you believe is true about their goal?
What do they believe they need to do to hit their goal? What do you believe they need to do?
How can you go deeper on this idea or a past topic: Why does it work this way? Why is XYZ happening?
Is it speaking UP to the type of clients you want to serve? Is this speaking to the person who is ready to buy? 

CONTENT:

When creating content, start by asking yourself these questions to help find clarity and step out of overthinking.

What is the purpose behind this piece of content?
What is the action/next step I want them to take?
What is the desire or goal they want to achieve?
What challenge is in the way?
What is the new belief or clarity that they need?
How can I help them create that belief or clarity?
What is missing? What can’t they see because they’re too close to it?
What can I document from my life, clients, and experience in my content?
How can I go deeper on an idea that my audience resonates with?
What other angles can I hit around the same theme?
