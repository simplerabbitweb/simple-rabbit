# Simple Rabbit — Offers and Pricing

## Overview

Simple Rabbit offers two consulting packages organized around the ACRR framework (Attract, Convert, Retain, Refer), plus website builds and care plans as a separate service line.

All consulting work focuses on private-pay patient acquisition for functional medicine, women's health, and concierge medicine practices.

---

## Consulting Offer 1: ACRR Growth Strategy

**Price:** $9,500–$12,500 (one-time project)

**Who it's for:** Practices that have steady patient volume but inconsistent growth. The practice is working but not compounding — no clear referral system, messaging that doesn't differentiate, or a website that talks to the wrong patient. Best fit for a practitioner who wants a clear plan and can execute with some support.

**What it includes:**

### Phase 1: Practice Audit (Week 1–2)
- Full ACRR audit across all four pillars
- Website review: messaging, SEO, conversion architecture
- Google Business Profile review
- Competitive landscape analysis (3–5 local competitors)
- Patient journey mapping (where does a new patient first find you? where do they drop off?)
- Intake form and onboarding review

### Phase 2: Strategy Deliverables (Week 2–4)
- ACRR Growth Strategy Report (30–50 pages): full findings and prioritized recommendations
- Ideal Patient Profile: one-page definition of the right-fit patient, including psychographics, search behavior, and messaging language
- Messaging Guide: homepage copy direction, headline options, brand voice notes specific to the practice
- Content Strategy: 12-month content calendar tied to search intent and ACRR pillars
- Local SEO Roadmap: specific keyword targets, citation audit, Google Business Profile optimization checklist

### Phase 3: Presentation and Handoff (Week 4)
- 90-minute strategy presentation call
- All deliverables delivered as editable documents
- 30-day follow-up support via email

**Outcome:** The practice owner knows exactly where patient acquisition is breaking down and has a prioritized, actionable plan to fix it. She can hand the strategy to her team or to a vendor (including Simple Rabbit) for execution.

## Post-Engagement Retainer

This is not a cold offer. It is only made available to clients who have already completed a Growth Strategy or Growth Accelerator engagement and want ongoing execution and accountability.

**Price:** $2,750–$4,500/month

**Who it's for:** Practices that have completed a Growth Strategy or Growth Accelerator and want ongoing execution and accountability. Also available to established practices that have a clear strategy but need someone to drive implementation month over month.

**What it includes (typical scope — customized per client):**

- Monthly ACRR progress review (30–45 min call)
- 2–4 blog posts per month (written to the practice's messaging guide and keyword targets)
- Google Business Profile management (post 2x/month, review response, photo updates)
- Email newsletter: 1–2 per month, written and sent via the practice's email platform
- Social content: 8–12 captions per month (practice reviews and approves before posting)
- Site maintenance: plugin updates, security monitoring, backup confirmation
- Small content edits (team bios, service descriptions, pricing updates)
- Monthly reporting: traffic, rankings, GBP impressions, form submissions

**What it does not include:**
- Paid advertising management
- Video production
- Full website rebuilds (those are scoped separately)

**Contract term:** 3-month minimum, then month-to-month.

## Pricing Philosophy

Simple Rabbit is not the cheapest option. The pricing reflects:
- Senior-level strategy work, not junior execution
- Time saved by the practitioner not having to manage vendors, briefs, or approvals
- The compounding value of consistent, strategic patient acquisition vs. one-off tactics
- A relationship model, not a transactional one

The right client is not price-shopping. She's buying certainty, trust, and time back.

---

## Sales and Proposal Notes

- Discovery call is 30–45 minutes, focused on the practice audit (not a pitch)
- Proposal delivered within 48 hours of call
- Proposals include one recommended package and one alternative (never three options — choice paralysis)
- Payment: 50% upfront, 50% at defined milestone (Growth Strategy: at strategy presentation; Growth Accelerator: at site launch)
- Contracts via DocuSign
- Invoicing via Stripe or direct bank transfer

---

## What to Say When Asked About Price

Don't lead with the number. Lead with the outcome.

"Before I give you a number, I want to understand where the practice is today and where it needs to get. The range is $9,500 to $28,000 depending on whether we're doing strategy only or strategy plus implementation. After a 30-minute call I can tell you exactly which one makes sense and why."

Never apologize for the price. Never offer a discount unprompted. The buyer who asks "is there a smaller option?" is the wrong buyer for this offer — direct her to the ACRR checklist and newsletter.
