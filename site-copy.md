## Who It's For
Who It's For

### This is built for practice owners whose work already speaks for itself.

My clients run private-pay women's wellness practices. They've built something specific, the kind of work patients seek out and pay for. The website should reflect that, before, during, and after the first appointment.

This is for you if…

- Your in-person experience is already high-quality, but the website doesn't show it
- You've outgrown your current site, or never had one worth having
- You want one person who handles it and stays on it, not a handoff to someone you've never met
- You treat the website like an investment, not an expense

## What's Included
What's Included

### A website built for your practice, not a template with your name on it.

**01 — Custom design, top to bottom**
No recycled layouts from other practices. Your site is built around your patients, your services, and how they actually search for what you do.

**02 — Strategic copywriting**
I write your pages while I design them, so the words and the layout shape each other. Every headline earns its place.

**03 — Local SEO foundations**
Service pages built to rank for what your patients actually search for. Plus a Google Business Profile setup that helps you show up in the local map.

**04 — Booking and conversion paths**
Clear ways for a patient to book, call, or message from every page. No dead ends. No "contact us" as the only option.

**05 — Ongoing care**
Hosting, security, backups, and up to 60 minutes of content changes every month. The site stays current without you touching it.

**06 — One point of contact**
You work directly with me. Not a project manager, account exec, or an offshore team. When you email, I answer.

Every site is built on WordPress, a platform you own and can grow with.

## How It Works
How It Works

### Three steps from first call to launched site.

**01 — Discovery and planning**
We start with a real conversation about your practice, your patients, and the website you actually need. From there, I map out the pages, the structure, and the strategy. You get a clear plan before any design work begins.

**02 — Copy, design, and build**
I write your pages, design them, and build the site, all of it. You see the work as it comes together, with check-ins along the way. Nothing gets handed off to a third party.

**03 — Launch and care**
We launch when the site is ready, not when a deadline says so. The day it goes live, your care plan starts. Hosting, security, updates, and content changes are handled from there.

## Common Questions
Common Questions

### What practice owners ask before booking.

**What kinds of practices do you work with?**
Private-pay women's wellness practices. Functional and integrative medicine, hormone and menopause care, holistic gynecology, aesthetics and laser, and adjacent specialties. If your patients pay out of pocket because the work is worth it, we're likely a fit.

**Can you write copy that actually sounds like my practice?**
Yes. I write every page before I design it, and I write to the actual patient you're trying to attract. The tone, the language, and the angle are all tailored to who your patients are and how they make decisions.

**Will my site show up on Google for the services I offer?**
That's the point. Every service page is written and structured to rank for what your patients are actually searching. SEO is baked in from day one, not bolted on later.

**Do you handle compliance, privacy, or industry-specific requirements?**
Marketing websites generally don't store sensitive patient data, so most compliance concerns (including HIPAA) live with your booking system, patient portal, or internal tools. If your practice has specific regulatory needs, we'll talk through them and make sure the site supports them.

**What if I already have a website or I'm launching something new?**
Both work. I rebuild existing sites that aren't pulling their weight, and I launch new practices from scratch. Fill out the contact form and we'll figure out which path makes sense for where you are.

**Can I edit the site myself after launch?**
You can, but most of my clients don't want to. That's what ongoing care is for. I handle the updates, the new service pages, the seasonal changes. You stay focused on your patients.
