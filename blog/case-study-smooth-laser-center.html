How Smooth Laser Center went from invisible online to getting clients from search every week

Client Overview
Client
Mona Perez, Smooth Laser Center
Location
Township of Washington, NJ
Industry
Laser hair removal & skincare services
Project
7-page custom WordPress website
Launched
July 2024

Mona Perez built Smooth Laser Center around a clear vision: a calm, sophisticated space where clients could get real results. Her studio in Township of Washington offers laser hair removal and skincare services, services that require trust, precision, and a provider who knows what she's doing. Mona had all of that.

Her website did not reflect any of it.

The old site was outdated and unprofessional, and it didn't even list all of her services. Someone searching for laser hair removal in her area would land on a page that gave them no reason to feel confident booking with her. The studio itself was polished and welcoming. The website looked like neither of those things.

The problem with an outdated website in a trust-based industry

Aesthetic and wellness services live or die on first impressions. Before a new client ever walks through the door, they have already decided whether or not they trust you, and that decision is made almost entirely online. They look at your website, they look at your photos, and they decide in seconds whether this feels like the kind of place they want to be.

An outdated website doesn't just look bad. It actively signals the wrong things. It tells potential clients that attention to detail is not a priority. That the experience might be inconsistent. That they can probably find someone who looks more established. In a category where the client is literally trusting you with their skin, those signals matter enormously.

Mona's work was excellent. Her space was beautiful. The website was sending none of that message, and she was losing clients to it before they ever had a chance to reach out.

What Simple Rabbit built

The project was a seven-page custom WordPress website, designed to match the quality and calm of the studio itself.

The design brief was clear from Mona's intake: minimalist, clean, and classy. Beige, white, and soft tones. Not busy. Not loud. The kind of site that makes you feel, before you've read a single word, that you are in good hands. That feeling is not an accident, it's a deliberate design outcome, and it requires every element of the page to be working together toward it.

Each service got its own dedicated space. Laser hair removal and skincare services attract different clients with different questions and different levels of familiarity. Lumping them together on a single generic services page does neither of them justice. Giving each service room to breathe, clear descriptions, what to expect, who it's for, lets the right client self-select and arrive already informed.

Smooth Laser Center, homepage

The site was also built with local SEO in mind from the first line of code. Schema markup, location-specific page structure, and keyword strategy targeted toward the clients in her area who are actively searching for these services. Not an afterthought. Built in from the start.

Smooth Laser Center on mobile

What happened after launch

The numbers tell a clear story.

In the period before the new site launched, Smooth Laser Center had 725 Google Search impressions and 28 clicks. In the roughly 20 months since launch, those numbers have grown to 4,322 impressions and 119 clicks, a nearly sixfold increase in visibility and a fourfold increase in traffic from search alone.

That growth is not the result of an ad campaign or a viral moment. It is the compounding result of a website built to be found. Pages structured correctly. Content that answers the questions people are actually typing into Google. A local presence that search engines can read and trust. It builds slowly at first and then it accelerates, which is exactly what has happened here.

And the search growth is only part of it. Every referral, every social media visitor, every person who hears about Smooth Laser Center and looks them up, they all land on a site that now confirms what they were told. It doesn't raise doubts. It raises confidence. That's the job.

"Leann was absolutely amazing! Brought my ideas and vision into life for a professional and sophisticated website! I get so many clients now from the website search! It's amazing! Thank you again Leann."

Mona Perez, Smooth Laser Center

An ongoing relationship, not a one-time project

About a year after launch, Mona moved into a new space, and her aesthetic had evolved with it. Because she's part of Simple Rabbit's monthly support program, we were able to step in and redesign several pages to align with the look and feel of her new office. No big project kick-off, no lengthy back-and-forth. We already knew the site inside and out, and the updates happened seamlessly.

That ongoing partnership also means Smooth Laser Center's promotions stay current. Seasonal offers, new specials, limited-time deals, we update them every month as part of the relationship. The site always reflects what's actually happening in the business, and Mona doesn't have to think about it.

Why this keeps happening

Mona’s results follow a pattern we see consistently. The quality of the work was already there. The client results were already real. What was missing was a website that communicated all of that clearly enough for a stranger to trust it within the first ten seconds of landing on the page.

When that gap closes, when the website finally matches the quality of the work being done, things start moving. Search rankings improve because the site is structured for it. Referrals convert more consistently because the site confirms the recommendation. New clients arrive already informed and already trusting, which means less time spent on the phone convincing people and more time doing the actual work.

That is what a website is supposed to do. And it does not require a massive marketing budget or a complicated strategy. It requires a site built correctly, with real intention, around the specific business it represents.
