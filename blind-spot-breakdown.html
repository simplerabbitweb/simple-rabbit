The blind spots I look for.

These are the things that quietly lose you patients, and the ones you’re least likely to catch on your own.

01

The path to book is buried.

You know exactly where the button is. A first-time visitor on her phone doesn’t, and she won’t go hunting for it.

02

The site talks about you, not her.

Your homepage leads with your credentials and your story. She’s scanning for whether you can help with what she’s dealing with right now.

03

You don’t show up for what people search.

You rank for your name. You don’t rank for the conditions and the town that bring new patients to Google in the first place.

04

It falls apart on a phone.

Most of your traffic is on mobile. If the menu, the forms, or the booking link are clumsy there, that’s where you’re losing people.

05

The words no longer match who you treat.

You’ve narrowed your focus since the site went up. The copy still speaks to the practice you used to run, not the one you have now.

06

It loads too slowly.

A few extra seconds and people leave before your offer ever loads. You don’t notice, because your browser already has the page cached.
