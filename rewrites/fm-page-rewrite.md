## 7. New FAQ Entry — Telehealth / Virtual Practice

**Add after the existing "I already have a functional medicine website" FAQ entry.**

**Question:**
> What if my practice sees patients virtually or across state lines?

**Answer:**
> A lot of functional medicine practices run on a hybrid model or see patients fully remotely. The site is built to reflect how your practice actually works, whether that's in-person only, a mix of in-person and virtual visits, or fully remote. Local SEO still matters for virtual practices because most patients search with location in mind even when they're open to telehealth. There's more on how that works specifically for practices without a physical office in [this piece on Google Business Profile for virtual practices](/blog/google-business-profile-virtual-practice).
