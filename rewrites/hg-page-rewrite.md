## 7. Add Patient Specificity — Problem Section

The Problem section currently describes the patient broadly. It's good writing, but it doesn't name the conditions she's searching for by name. Adding condition-specific language gives Google more signals and gives the right-fit patient a clearer mirror.

**Current last paragraph of the Problem section:**
> The patient who finds a holistic gynecology practice through search has usually been looking for a while. She wants integrative care and she wants it from someone with real clinical training. She can read a site in thirty seconds and know whether the practice understands what she's looking for. The site that fails to signal both competence and philosophy loses her before she reaches the contact page. Holistic gynecology website design sits between two established templates, and neither one gets the balance right.

**Proposed — add a new paragraph before this one:**
> She's usually researching something specific. PCOS that hasn't responded to the pill-and-move-on approach. Endometriosis she's been told she just has to live with. Perimenopause symptoms her OB dismissed as normal. She knows what the conventional answer is, and she doesn't want it. What she's looking for is a clinician who will take the full picture seriously.

**Why:** Names the conditions patients actually search by name. Helps Google connect this page to those searches. Gives the right-fit patient language she recognizes from her own experience. Doesn't change the design or layout — this paragraph drops in before the existing closing paragraph.
