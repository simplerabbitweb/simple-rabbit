Common questions

Morris County web design, answered.

Do you work with businesses in Morris County?

Yes. We work with service businesses throughout Morris County, including Morristown, Parsippany, Chatham, Madison, Denville, Randolph, Mendham, and all surrounding towns.

What does a website project cost?

The total investment depends on scope, number of pages, and whether you want ongoing support. We give you a clear number upfront, no surprises.

How is Simple Rabbit different from a national agency?

We serve clients throughout Morris County, NJ. We understand the expectations of clients in towns like Morristown, Chatham, and Mendham, and we know how to build for them. You work directly with Leann Frank, the founder, from start to finish — no handoffs, no junior designers.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. The entire process works remotely and is designed to respect your time. We handle everything through a simple, structured workflow — no unnecessary meetings, no back-and-forth confusion.

What industries do you work with?

We specialize in service businesses: home organizers, coaches, consultants, attorneys, wellness providers, interior designers, event planners, and other professionals whose clients need to trust them before reaching out.

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects are complete in 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff. Timeline depends on scope and how quickly we can exchange content and feedback.
