The work

Bring nature indoors, one wall at a time

Every surface is mixed, tinted and troweled on site, so the finish suits the light in your room rather than a sample in a showroom. Lauren works alongside builders and designers from the first coat to the final seal — and stays in touch long after the drop sheets come up.

Bathrooms, kitchens, feature walls, whole homes and commercial fitouts, finished in microcement, Venetian plaster, Marmorino and lime wash.

About Lauren

An artist's hand, a tradesperson's preparation

Lauren Goodwin is a decorative painter based in Mayfield East. Her career began in New York, applying design work across film, retail and interiors. In 2018 she founded Lauren Goodwin Decorative Finishes, bringing together art, architecture and the textures she grew up around.
