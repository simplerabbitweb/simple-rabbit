Turning walls into Works of Art

Bodoni Moda — current

On the italic. The hero sets "Works of Art" in italic, and four of these faces have no true italic — the browser fakes one by slanting the roman, which looks cheap at that size. They're marked below. Picking one of them means changing the hero treatment, not just the font.

Once you've chosen, it's a two-line change in the theme: the Google Fonts URL in functions.php and the --display token in style.css. Sizes may need a nudge — Cormorant and Italiana run small, Cinzel and Marcellus run large.
