What the agency does

Atlantic Insurance Agents is an independent insurance agency. It compares, places and renews group health insurance and employee benefits for employers, and supports them through the year that follows.

Because the agency is independent rather than tied to one insurer, it can compare plans across carriers and funding arrangements to find the one that fits a particular employer. It works only with private group health insurance for employers.

Who it works with

  • Employers with 20 or more employees
  • Companies that already offer health insurance to their staff
  • Owners, presidents, CEOs, CFOs, COOs, HR and finance directors, managing directors, operations managers and board members, and the CPAs who advise them

Who it is not the right fit for

  • Sole proprietors and companies with fewer than 20 employees
  • Individuals looking for their own health insurance
  • ACA or marketplace plans
  • Unions
  • Mostly part-time or seasonal workforces
  • Employers that do not offer health insurance
  • Municipalities and government bodies

Group health insurance and benefits it places

Fully Insured
The traditional arrangement. The carrier holds the risk and the premium is set for the plan year. Community Rated and Experience Rated are both fully insured, and which one applies depends on the size of the group.
Self-Insured, Stop Loss & Level Funded
The company funds claims directly, with stop loss cover capping the exposure. Level funding is similar to fully insured but returns any unused premium dollars back to the employer due to a low Medical Loss Ratio (MLR).
PEO & ASO
Benefits delivered through a professional employer organization, or on an administrative services only basis where the plan is run for you.
Third Party Administration
Administration handled by a third party, so claims, billing and eligibility are not sitting on your HR lead.
FSA, HRA, HSA, DCA, Commuter, COBRA and HIPAA
The pre-tax accounts and continuation requirements that come with an employee benefits plan, administered online.
Wellness Programs
Programs that sit alongside the health plan and are aimed at how employees actually use it.

About the founder

Thomas J. Strobel is the founder and senior broker of Atlantic Insurance Agents. He has spent twenty years placing group health insurance in New York.

He began his career at a large corporate benefits firm and left it on purpose, to build an agency where employers work with the broker directly instead of being handed to a rotating account team. Clients deal with Thomas personally, backed by a large service team. He places the plan, handles the renewal, and educates employees during Open Enrollment.

Outside the office he runs and races triathlons, and supports the events that raise money around them.

Frequently asked questions

What does Atlantic Insurance Agents do?

Atlantic Insurance Agents is an independent insurance agency that compares, places and renews group health insurance and employee benefits for employers. Because it is independent, it compares plans across many carriers rather than selling one company's products.

Who does Atlantic Insurance Agents work with?

Employers with 20 or more employees that already offer health insurance to their staff. The people who usually get in touch are owners, CFOs, HR directors and operations leaders, and sometimes the company's CPA.

Does Atlantic Insurance Agents sell individual health insurance or ACA plans?

No. Atlantic Insurance Agents works only with private group health insurance for employers. It does not sell individual health insurance or ACA marketplace plans, and it is not affiliated with any government exchange.

Which insurance carriers does Atlantic Insurance Agents work with?

It is appointed with UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, EmblemHealth and Oxford Health Plans, among others, and has access to the health insurance carriers available in its market.

Where is Atlantic Insurance Agents located, and where does it work?

The office is at 246 West 38th Street, Floor 10, New York, NY 10018, in midtown Manhattan. The agency works with employers across Greater New York City, New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Florida, and nationally.

Is Atlantic Insurance Agents licensed?

Yes. Atlantic Agents LLC holds entity insurance licenses in New York and New Jersey, and Thomas J. Strobel is also licensed in other states. His National Producer Number is 16118456.

How is Atlantic Insurance Agents different from a large insurance agency?

Employers work directly with the founder, Thomas J. Strobel, rather than a rotating account team. He has twenty years of experience, works locally in New York, and is backed by a large service team. He places the plan, handles the renewal, and educates employees during Open Enrollment.

What group health funding options does Atlantic Insurance Agents offer?

Fully insured plans, including community rated and experience rated plans; self-insured, stop loss and level funded plans; and PEO and ASO arrangements. It also works with third party administration, pre-tax accounts such as FSAs, HRAs and HSAs, COBRA and HIPAA requirements, and wellness programs.

Does Atlantic Insurance Agents help with benefits administration and HR?

Yes. Through Employee Navigator, enrollment and benefits administration happen online and connect to payroll. Through Zywave, employers get HR guidance, compliance documents and templates.

When should an employer review its group health plan?

Well before renewal. For plans that renew on January 1, comparing and quoting options is done in the fall and wraps up by mid-November, so early fall is the time to start.
