A note from our founder

I'm the founder and senior broker at Atlantic Insurance Agents.

I started out at a large corporate benefits shop and left on purpose. The accounts were big and the resources were real, but the person who sold the plan was rarely the person who picked up the phone six months later.

Here you work with me. I place the plan, I handle the renewal, and I take the call when something goes wrong during open enrollment. There is no account team to get passed to.

My team helps where it makes sense. The relationship stays mine, and so does the outcome.

Thomas
