How it works

Four steps, and three of them are ours.

STEP 01

We go get your list

Give us a login to your CRM or send a CSV. We clean it up, pull the dead addresses, kill the duplicates, and authenticate your domain so you land in the inbox instead of the junk folder.

STEP 02

We build the campaign

Before anybody writes a word, we work out what you actually want to sell this season, who should hear about it, and what happens when somebody bites. That's the part still working in month eight.

STEP 03

We write it and design it

Someone learns your business, your service area, and the jobs you actually want more of. Then it gets written the way you'd talk in a driveway, and laid out in your colors instead of dropped into a stock template.

STEP 04

You approve, we run it

The draft comes to you. Change anything, or hit approve. We send it, watch who does what, and adjust the next one based on what actually moved.

Fair questions

The stuff people ask on the first call.

Six months is a real commitment. Why?

It is, and we'd rather say why up front than bury it in a contract. The first email to a list that's gone cold doesn't do much. The fourth one does. Automations need time to catch people in the week they're actually ready, and click data doesn't tell you anything true until enough people have clicked. A thirty-day test would only prove that thirty days isn't enough. Six months is about where we can put a number in front of you and defend it. If we just wanted your card on file, we'd sell you a monthly plan and let you quit in week three the way everybody else does.

What's Flodesk and why do I need it?

It's the platform the whole thing runs on. You pay Flodesk directly, and our link takes 25% off your first year. We put the account in your name on purpose. The list is yours, the automations are yours, and if you ever decide you're done with us you keep all of it and it keeps running. Nobody holds your customer list hostage, including us.

I don't really have a list.

You almost certainly do. Anybody who's ever booked a job, asked for a quote, or paid an invoice is on it somewhere. We've pulled 1,400 usable addresses out of a shop that swore they had none. If it turns out you genuinely have nothing, we'll tell you that on the call and we won't take your money.

Won't my customers get annoyed and unsubscribe?

A few will, and that's fine. The ones who go were never calling you again anyway. What annoys people is getting a sales blast twice a week with nothing in it for them, so we don't write those. Two emails a month that are actually useful is closer to a good neighbor than a pest.

Isn't all of this done with AI now?

A lot of it is, and you can tell instantly when you read it. Your customers can too. We named the company after the one thing we won't do. A person who knows your business writes every email that goes out with your name on it.

I already post on Facebook.

Facebook shows your post to maybe five percent of the people who follow you, and only if the algorithm feels like it that day. An email lands in the inbox of everybody you send it to. You own the list, and nobody can take it away or start charging you for it.

How fast will I see anything?

First email usually goes out inside two weeks, most of which is us cleaning your list and getting your domain set up to send. Jobs tend to show up over the following month, and they're rarely the people you'd have guessed. The automations are the slower half. Those build on themselves, and by month four they're working on people you haven't thought about in a year.

What do you need from me?

One call at the start so we understand the business, access to your customer list, a Flodesk account in your name, and about ten minutes a month to read a draft and say yes. That's the whole job.
