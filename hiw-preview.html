How It Works

Three steps from first call to launched system.

01

Discovery and strategy

I start with a real conversation about your practice, your patients, and the system you actually need. From there, I map out the strategy: how patients will find you, what your website has to do, and how email keeps them engaged.

02

Build and consult

Depending on your package, I build the pieces that need building — the website, the email workflow, the conversion paths — and consult on the rest.

03

Launch and care

I launch when the site is ready, not when a deadline says so. The day it goes live, your care plan starts.
