## The client (facts)
- **Name:** Dr. David Deramo (pronounced "Dramo"), board-certified orthopedic surgeon.
- **Practice:** Deramo Orthopaedics & Wellness Center.
- **Location:** West New York, NJ (Hudson County — just north of Jersey City, on the Hudson across from Manhattan).
- **Experience:** ~20 years practicing; opened his own boutique practice ~2 years ago.
- **What he does:** Orthopedic surgery + sports medicine (arthroscopic shoulder & knee; focus on shoulder, knee, hip), personal injury, workers' comp.
- **Business model:** Out-of-network by choice; referral-driven.
- **The pivot:** Standing up a wellness center around two machines he already owns — **Emsculpt Neo** (body contouring + FDA-cleared functional/rehab use) and **Emvital medical laser** (pain & inflammation). Wellness planned to expand later into GLP-1s, peptides, hormones, functional medicine once a nurse practitioner is on board.

---

## 1. Before — the situation
- Old site was **"a box to check," not a tool.** Built years ago only because some companies wouldn't sign a BAA without a website — basically proof he was a legit operation.
- **"Basic,"** little to no SEO. He wasn't even sure his site would come up if you Googled his actual name.
- Nothing about it drove patients — and for years that was fine, because the business ran on **relationships**: attorneys, chiropractors, pain-management doctors, case managers referring him from dinners and networking. The site never had to pull weight.
- **What changed:** he fast-forwarded the wellness center after buying ~**$600,000** of equipment. He didn't want the machines sitting idle the way they had at his former practice. Now he needed a site that could actually *attract* wellness patients and look the part.
- **Frustrations he named:**
  - Site wasn't sophisticated/eye-catching enough for where the practice is heading.
  - No real SEO or keyword targeting.
  - No page structure to rank for the individual services he offers.
  - Wants **organic search, not paid ads** — feels people click off ads fast.

---

## 2. The work (what Leann did)
**Signed scope:** Full SEO-focused website rebuild — **$8,000**, ~4 weeks of writing + build, split into two $4,000 payments (signing + launch). Plus **$149/mo care plan** and an **$850 blog add-on** (4 posts).

**Build covered:**
- Strategy + a **sitemap built for SEO**.
- **All copywriting** (Leann wrote everything; he supplied only photos).
- A **separate page per core service** so each can rank for its own keyword.
- Modern, **mobile-friendly** design + build.
- On-page SEO basics: titles, meta, internal linking, local signals.
- Launch support.

**Beyond the site:**
- Optimized his **Google Business Profile**.
- Blog positioned as an **organic-traffic engine**; keyword blogging offered as the lever for more local visibility.

**Site structure — 11 pages:**
1. Home
2. About Dr. Deramo
3. Orthopedic Care & Sports Medicine (hub) → 4 service pages: **Shoulder, Knee, Hip, Sports Medicine**
4. Personal Injury
5. Workers' Comp
6. Wellness Center (single page at launch)
7. Blog
8. Contact

**Smart moves worth highlighting in the write-up:**
- Each service page built for **its own keyword**.
- **Messaging tailored by audience** — Personal Injury and Workers' Comp pages lead with what **attorneys and case managers** care about (his referral engine): personalized reports he writes himself, direct access to him, notes turned around in **under 24 hours**.
- Captured his **brand system**: black & white with deep purple, mid purple, bright blue; **Futura** headlines, **Roboto** body. Planned **two homepage directions** to settle the look (his brand sheet and reference sites pulled different moods).

**Quality-control layer (a real differentiator — most builds skip this):**
- **Two structured staging QA passes** before showing him the site.
- Caught: broken CTAs, footer link problems, a **medical-accuracy issue where the site over-claimed arthroscopic hip surgery**, **HIPAA questions on patient testimonials**, and brand/copy inconsistencies — then re-checked the fixes.

**Phase 2 (already scoped):**
- Split Wellness into **individually-ranking pages**.
- Add **GLP-1s, peptides, hormones, and a functional-medicine hub**.
