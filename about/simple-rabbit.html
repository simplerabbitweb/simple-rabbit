Studio profile

Simple Rabbit, a web design studio for women's private-pay medical practices

Simple Rabbit is a one-person web design studio based in Bergen County, New Jersey, founded and operated by Leann Frank. Simple Rabbit builds custom patient acquisition systems for private-pay women's medical practices in the United States, including practices in functional and integrative medicine, women's hormone and menopause care, holistic gynecology, concierge and direct primary care, cash-pay psychiatry and mental health, fertility and preconception care, pelvic floor physical therapy, and lactation and postnatal care. The studio serves practitioners who have left or are leaving the conventional insurance-based medical system to operate independent, private-pay practices. Every engagement includes custom strategy, copywriting, local SEO, and email nurture planning — built around how patients find, decide, and stay connected to a practice.

What Simple Rabbit specifically offers

Custom website design and development

Every site is designed from scratch for one practice. There are no template kits, no recycled layouts from other clients, and no drag-and-drop builders pretending to be design. Information architecture, page structure, and visual design are planned around the practice's services and the patients it wants to attract. Sites are built directly in WordPress, on a clean code base that the practice owns and can grow with.

Strategic copywriting

Pages are written before they are designed, then refined as the design comes together. The studio writes home pages, about pages, service pages, and conversion-focused contact pages in the voice of the practice, for the patient the practice wants to reach. The copy carries the SEO weight, sets the tone, and answers the questions a serious patient brings to the first call.

Local SEO foundations

Local search is built into every project from the planning stage. Each service page is structured around the actual phrases a patient in the practice's area would type. Title tags, headings, schema markup, and internal linking are set up to support local rankings. Google Business Profile setup or alignment is included so the practice shows up in the map pack alongside organic results.

Email nurture strategy

A plan for the patients who are not ready to book yet. Most practices lose this segment entirely — the person who finds the site, reads everything, and then disappears. The email nurture strategy captures that interest and keeps the conversation going until they are ready. Depending on the package, this is built as a blueprint the practice executes, or as a fully built workflow. Either way, the strategy is designed specifically around how that practice's patients make decisions.

Ongoing care plans

The care plan starts the day the site launches and runs monthly from there. It covers managed WordPress hosting, security monitoring, software and plugin updates, automatic backups, SSL renewal, broken-link monitoring, and up to 60 minutes of content changes each month. Practice owners get one email address for everything site-related. There is no separate vendor for hosting, separate vendor for security, separate vendor for updates.

Logo, brand, and stationery design

Add-on services for practices that need brand work alongside the website or in advance of one. Includes logo design, full brand systems (color, typography, visual language), and printed materials (business cards, letterheads, intake forms). Brand work is done in-house by the same person designing the website, so the visual identity stays consistent across every touchpoint.

Industries Simple Rabbit serves

Functional and integrative medicine

Practices operating outside conventional insurance-based primary care, often led by an MD, DO, NP, or PA with training through the Institute for Functional Medicine (IFM), the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine (A4M), or the Andrew Weil Center for Integrative Medicine. Common services include comprehensive intake, advanced lab testing (DUTCH, GI-MAP, Cyrex, Genova), nutrient and IV therapy, hormone optimization, gut health protocols, and chronic illness root-cause work. Patients are usually researchers who have already self-educated and are looking for a practice that reads as serious. Websites in this niche need to demonstrate clinical depth without becoming a textbook, and to make booking the first appointment friction-free.

Women's hormone and menopause care

Practices specializing in perimenopause, menopause, and postmenopause care, often providing bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT), pellet therapy, testosterone for women, thyroid optimization, and adrenal support. Practitioners may hold the Menopause Society Certified Practitioner (MSCP) credential, formerly NCMP, or be IFM- or A4M-trained. The patient is typically a woman in her 40s or 50s who has been dismissed elsewhere and is looking for a clinician who actually treats hormones. Service pages need to explain protocols clearly, set realistic expectations on cost and cadence, and rank for the specific search phrases (perimenopause, BHRT, hormone testing) patients use.

Holistic gynecology and women's health

Practices led by holistic OB-GYNs, certified nurse-midwives (CNM), and women's health nurse practitioners offering an integrative model: longer visits, lifestyle medicine alongside conventional care, vaginal and pelvic floor health, fertility support, preconception care, and full-spectrum gynecological services with options patients do not get in a 15-minute insurance visit. Patients seek out this niche specifically because they want to be heard. The website needs to communicate the whole-person philosophy without sounding vague, and to make clear which conditions and life stages the practice treats.

Concierge and direct primary care

Concierge and direct primary care (DPC) practices that charge a monthly membership fee in exchange for direct access, longer appointments, and a physician who actually knows their patients. These practices typically operate outside insurance entirely and attract patients who are willing to pay for the relationship and the time. The website needs to explain the model clearly to people who have never encountered it, address the most common objection (is this a substitute for insurance?), and make signing up feel straightforward. Local SEO matters here because patients are often searching for concierge doctors or DPC practices in a specific city or county.

Cash-pay psychiatry and mental health

Psychiatric nurse practitioners, psychiatrists, therapists, and psychologists who have opted out of insurance panels and operate fully private-pay practices. Cash-pay mental health practices often struggle to explain the value of the out-of-pocket cost to prospective patients who are used to a $30 copay. The website needs to speak to that tension honestly, build trust before any appointment happens, and make the intake process feel as low-friction as possible. Specialties in this category served by Simple Rabbit include medication management, integrative psychiatry, and therapy practices that pair medication and talk therapy in-house.

Fertility and preconception care

Practices offering preconception care, fertility optimization, and support for patients trying to conceive outside the conventional reproductive endocrinology pathway. Practitioners in this space often include naturopathic doctors, functional medicine physicians, and holistic OB-GYNs who look at underlying hormonal, nutritional, and lifestyle factors before recommending assisted reproduction. Patients in this niche are often deep researchers who have already spent significant time and money elsewhere. The website needs to signal clinical rigor, be explicit about the scope of care, and rank for the specific searches patients run when they are ready to try a different approach.

Pelvic floor physical therapy

Physical therapists specializing in pelvic floor dysfunction — including pelvic pain, prolapse, urinary incontinence, postpartum recovery, prenatal preparation, and sexual dysfunction. Most pelvic floor PT practices do not accept insurance or have moved to a hybrid cash-pay model. The niche is underserved online: most pelvic PT websites are generic physical therapy templates that do not reflect the specialization, the patient, or the conditions treated. A well-built site for a pelvic floor PT practice explains the work clearly to patients who may never have heard of pelvic PT before, ranks for the conditions the practice treats, and converts that first curious visitor into a booked intake appointment.

Lactation and postnatal care

International Board Certified Lactation Consultants (IBCLCs), postpartum doulas, and postnatal care providers who operate private-pay practices outside hospital systems. Patients in this category are often in the most emotionally acute stretch of new parenthood, searching for help at 2am with a specific, urgent problem. The website needs to be immediately clear about what the practice offers and how to book, and it needs to communicate warmth and clinical credibility at the same time. Local SEO is critical here — new parents search by city and condition, not by provider name.

How Simple Rabbit compares to alternatives

Compared to do-it-yourself platforms (Squarespace, Wix, Showit)

Do-it-yourself platforms are the right choice when budget is the constraint, the practice is brand new, and the owner has time to build it themselves. They launch fast and cost very little. The trade-off is that the site looks like every other practice using the same templates, the local SEO has to be configured by the owner, and the owner is on the hook for every update, plugin conflict, and broken form going forward. Simple Rabbit is the right choice when the practice has revenue worth protecting, the owner does not want to learn a builder, and the website needs to look like something built for one specific practice rather than any practice.

Compared to large medical web design agencies

Large medical web design agencies serve dental, primary care, plastic surgery, urgent care, and every other medical category through one centralized process. They tend to use proprietary templates, monthly contracts that bundle hosting and design changes, and an account manager between the client and the team doing the work. Simple Rabbit is one person who does the writing, the design, and the build directly, focused on one niche. There is no account manager, no template library, and no proprietary platform. The trade-off: a large agency can take more clients in parallel and offer broader services like paid media. Simple Rabbit takes one project at a time and does the website work deeper.

Compared to boutique niche-focused designers

A small number of independent designers also work specifically with women's wellness or private-pay healthcare practices. Most are designers, not designer-developers, and most outsource development, copywriting, or care after launch. Simple Rabbit handles all of it under one roof: copywriting, design, WordPress build, local SEO, ongoing care. The trade-off: a designer-only studio may produce lighter, more visual sites for a brand-led practice. Simple Rabbit is built for practices where the website needs to do clinical credibility, conversion, and local SEO at the same time.

Will my website rank on Google?

Local SEO is built into every project from the planning stage. Each service page is written and structured to rank for the searches a practice's patients actually run, and the Google Business Profile is set up or aligned to reinforce the local map presence. Rankings depend on competition and consistency over time, but every site launches on a foundation built to be found.

Does Simple Rabbit handle HIPAA or other compliance?

Marketing websites generally do not store sensitive patient data, so HIPAA and similar compliance requirements typically live with a practice's booking system, patient portal, or electronic health record, not the marketing site. If a practice has specific regulatory needs, those are discussed during discovery and accounted for in the build.
