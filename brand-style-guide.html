07 — Voice and Tone

Three words: Direct. Warm. Quietly confident. All three at once. Direct without warm is cold. Warm without direct is soft. Confident without warm is arrogant.

Write this

Short sentences. Then a slightly longer one. Then short again.Vary length, lean short
Specific nouns over generic ones."A menopause specialist" not "a healthcare provider"
Concrete verbs over abstract ones."Ranks on Google" not "achieves visibility"
Active voice throughout."We build custom sites" not "Custom sites are built"
Name the problem plainly."Your contact form isn't visible on mobile"
One idea per sentence.If it has three commas, it's probably two ideas

Not this

Em-dashes anywhere.Hard rule. Use a comma, period, or colon instead.
Triple adjective lists."Clean, intentional, and strategic" — one is sharper.
"Not just X, but Y" constructions.An AI tell. Just say the thing.
Spa/coach language.Elevate, transform, journey, manifest, soul-led
Borrowed proof points.No client results that don't exist yet
"Cash-pay" anywhere.Always: private-pay

On brand

I help functional and precision medicine doctors get new patients consistently. Not by running ads or building a social following. By fixing the parts of their patient acquisition that are broken.

Off brand

I empower visionary practitioners to unlock their full patient acquisition potential through transformative, comprehensive, and holistic digital strategies that elevate their online presence.
