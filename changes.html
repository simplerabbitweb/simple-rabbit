Website Care Plan

Need a change to your website?

Fill out the form via the link and we’ll get to it within 3 business days.

Submit a change request →

What’s included

Up to 60 minutes of content updates per month. This covers things like:

  • Text edits and copy changes
  • Swapping out photos or images
  • Adding new info to existing pages
  • Updating hours, contact info, or pricing
  • Small layout tweaks to existing pages

What’s not included

Anything beyond 60 minutes or beyond a content update. For example:

  • New pages
  • Design changes or layout rework
  • New features or functionality
  • Custom development work
  • Anything requiring outside vendors

If your request falls outside the care plan, we’ll reach out with a quote before doing any work. Nothing gets billed without your okay.

Turnaround

Most requests are completed within 3 business days.

Mark something as High priority only if your business is actually impacted — like a broken link or wrong phone number on the site.
